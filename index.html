⚠️ Fasting Cautions

Crohn's Disease (Primary)

Fast during remission only. Stop if flare symptoms appear. Stick to 16–24 hr; reserve 36+ hr for confirmed stable remission. Hydrate 3+ L/day. LMNT electrolytes critical. Bone broth (4–8 oz) acceptable if energy crashes. Track symptoms in a journal.

Electrolyte Depletion

Past 16 hrs without electrolytes risks cramps, headaches, palpitations. Use LMNT packets. Critical on Sulfasalazine. Break fast immediately if dizzy or irregular heartbeat.

Medication Timing

Sulfasalazine (3 g/day, 8 AM + 4 PM) still taken during fasts with water. Consult gastroenterologist for 36+ hr adjustments. Never skip meds for a fast.

    22Why Does Time Speed Up As You Age?

    At age 10, one year is 10% of your life. At age 50, it's 2%. Your brain encodes time proportionally to lived experience — a phenomenon called "proportional theory." The brain also physically slows — neural processing speed decreases, and fewer new neural connections form, meaning fewer "frames" are captured per unit of time. Children's brains capture vastly more sensory frames per second than adult brains.
    Practice: Seek novelty daily — new routes, new foods, new skills, travel. Break routine deliberately. Mindfulness and presence expand subjective time by increasing frame density. The Science tab itself is an act of learning that slows perceived time.
